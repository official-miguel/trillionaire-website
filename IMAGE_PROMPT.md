# Image generation brief — Trillionaire Designs service pages

Generate one hero image per service page for a premium Kenyan software studio's
website. These images must NOT look like generic AI-generated stock art —
no glossy 3D robots, no generic "digital brain" clichés, no purple-blue gradient
tech backgrounds, no stock-photo business people. The site's whole identity is
built on restraint: think Apple / Linear / Stripe-grade editorial precision,
not sci-fi hype.

## Brand system (must match exactly)

- **Colors**: rich near-black background (#0A0A0A), pure white, and signature
  lime green (#32CD32) as the one recurring accent across every image.
- **Per-page accent** (use IN ADDITION to lime, as the secondary color for
  that page only):
  - Web development: electric blue #2E6BFF
  - App development: cyan #00C2D1
  - Systems development: violet #7C5CFF
  - AI: magenta #FF3EA5
  - AI training: amber #FFB020
- **Style**: Swiss/editorial minimalism. Flat or subtly dimensional vector-style
  compositions, thin precise line work, generous negative space, sharp geometry.
  No noise textures, no lens flares, no photorealistic renders, no drop-shadow
  heavy skeuomorphism, no clutter.
- **Mood**: premium, confident, engineered — like a high-end product shot, not
  a mood board. Should feel handcrafted and specific to each service, not an
  interchangeable "tech background."
- **Aspect ratio**: 16:10 (landscape), high resolution (at least 1600x1000px),
  transparent or solid #0A0A0A background so it drops cleanly into a dark UI panel.

## Per-page briefs

1. **Web development** — An abstract browser window / webpage layout composition:
   clean wireframe-style rectangles suggesting a modern website layout (nav bar,
   hero block, content grid), rendered as glowing outlined shapes in electric
   blue and lime against black. Feels fast and precise, like a blueprint for a
   website coming to life.

2. **App development** — An abstract mobile phone silhouette with a minimal
   app interface inside (stacked card elements, a bottom nav dot), glowing in
   cyan and lime line-work against black. Should suggest motion/interactivity
   without literal icons or logos.

3. **Systems development** — A hub-and-spoke or network diagram: a central
   node connected to several satellite modules by clean lines, like an
   architecture diagram elevated into art. Violet and lime glowing lines
   against black. Suggests interconnected, orchestrated systems (like a school
   management platform coordinating many modules).

4. **AI** — A precise grid or lattice of small nodes/dots with one clear focal
   point (e.g. a checkmark or resolving pattern) glowing in magenta and lime.
   Should read as "structured intelligence," not a generic glowing brain or
   humanoid robot.

5. **AI training** — An abstract graduation-cap or open-book motif rendered in
   thin geometric line art, glowing in amber and lime against black. Should
   feel like knowledge transfer, not a literal classroom photo.

## Output

Five separate images, named:
`web-development.png`, `app-development.png`, `systems.png`, `ai.png`, `training.png`

Deliver at 1600x1000px minimum, PNG with transparent or #0A0A0A background.
