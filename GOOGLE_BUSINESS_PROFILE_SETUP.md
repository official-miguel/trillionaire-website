## 11. GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE INTEGRATION

### Add Your Website to Google Search Console:
1. Visit: https://search.google.com/search-console/
2. Add property: `trillionairedesigns.co.ke`
3. Verify ownership using one of these methods:
   - **HTML file upload** (recommended)
   - **HTML meta tag** (already prepared in your website)
   - **Google Analytics**
   - **DNS record**

### Verification Meta Tag:
When you get your verification code from Google Search Console, add it to your website layout:

```typescript
// In src/app/layout.tsx under metadata
verification: {
  google: 'YOUR-VERIFICATION-CODE-HERE',
}
```

### Submit Your Sitemap:
1. In Google Search Console, go to "Sitemaps"
2. Submit: `https://trillionairedesigns.co.ke/sitemap.xml`

## 14. COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Don't:**
- Use a P.O. Box as your business address
- Stuff keywords into your business name (e.g., "Trillionaire Designs Web Design Kenya")
- Create multiple profiles for the same business
- Buy fake reviews
- Ignore negative reviews
- Use low-quality or stock photos

✅ **Do:**
- Keep information consistent across all platforms
- Use real business photos
- Respond to reviews authentically
- Keep your profile updated
- Use natural language in descriptions
